### Foundation Contractor (Traditional)
- **Phase 3:** Foundation *(Traditional foundation)*
- **Phase 4:** Under-Slab Services *(Under-slab prep, vapor barrier)*
- **Construction Methods:** Traditional-Frame, SIP

### ICF Installer ⭐ *ICF-Specific*
- **Phase 3:** ICF Foundation *(ICF forms, concrete for ICF walls)*
- **Phase 4:** Under-Slab Services *(Under-slab prep)*
- **Construction Methods:** ICF only
- **Special Items:** ICF forms and materials (#152), Concrete for ICF walls (#153)

### Post-Frame Foundation Contractor ⭐ *Post-Frame-Specific*
- **Phase 3:** Foundation *(Simpler foundation - piers/pads for posts)*
- **Construction Methods:** Post-Frame only
- **Note:** Much lighter foundation requirements than traditional

### Concrete Contractor (All Methods)
- **Phase 3:** Foundation *(Footings, walls, slabs - varies by method)*
- **Phase 6:** Concrete Slabs & Flatwork *(Garage slab, driveway, sidewalks, patio)*
- **All Construction Methods:** Same for flatwork

## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTES**

### Multi-Phase Contractors (Send ONE bid request covering multiple phases):
- **Plumber:** Under-Slab → Rough-In → Kitchen/Bath Finish
- **Electrician:** Under-Slab → Rough-In → Finish → Final
- **HVAC:** Rough-In → Finish

### Construction Method-Specific Contractors:
- **Traditional-Frame:** Standard framing contractor, truss company
- **Post-Frame:** Post-frame builder (includes shell, often metal siding)
- **ICF:** ICF installer (specialized training for ICF forms/concrete)
- **SIP:** SIP installer (factory panels, specialized crew)
- **Modular:** Modular dealer + crane operator (factory-built home)

### Sequential Dependencies:
- Foundation Contractor → Concrete Contractor (often same company)
- Framing → Roofing → Exterior (strict sequence)
- Rough-In trades (Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC) → Insulation → Drywall

### Specialty Considerations:
- **Phase 17 (Kitchen & Bath)** involves 5+ different contractor types
- **Phase 4 (Under-Slab)** needs coordination with Plumbing, Electrical, Foundation
- **Phase 8 (Exterior)** may be split among multiple specialty contractors
- **Post-Frame & Modular:** Fewer exterior contractors needed (included in package)
- **SIP:** Reduced insulation needs (built into panels)

### Bid Strategy by Construction Method:
- **Traditional-Frame:** Most contractor types needed (~30)
- **Post-Frame:** Fewer contractors, shell is package deal (~25)
- **ICF:** Specialty foundation, otherwise similar to traditional (~28)
- **SIP:** Specialty framing, reduced insulation (~27)
- **Modular:** Fewest contractors, home is pre-built (~20)
